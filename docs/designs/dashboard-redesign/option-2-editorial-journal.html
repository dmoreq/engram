Reading notes

  • est marks a figure whose field carries a *_estimated flag — saved_tokens, graph_saved_tokens, preventable_loc, avg_callers. Solid figures are measured on the sampled floor (detector_counts is counted on the sampled candidate list, not the corpus).
  • Data-age chip: production binds age = floor(now − generated_at) and resets it on every successful /api/summary refetch; this mockup simulates generated_at at page load.
  • The wire window is the latest 50 retained entries; no pagination or time-range query exists server-side yet.
  • Live arrivals in this mockup are simulated; production appends arrive from /events.
  • Demo-shaped filler, disclosed per section: dispatch-roster values, ledger rows beyond app, floor-preview rows beyond the featured twin, pre-write dispatch rows beyond the first, and anchor rows beyond the sample note.
  • Theme follows prefers-color-scheme; the toggle persists to localStorage. Production embedding moves this style and script into the include_str! assets under a CSP without unsafe-inline.
